Elections

Elections for Robbinsdale's Mayor and City Council are held in even years. The Mayor and City Council Members serve four-year terms.

  1. Upcoming Elections

  • Early Voting/Direct Balloting starts October 16

What's on the ballot:

  • State General Races
  • Robbinsdale Ward 3 and Ward 4 Council Member General Races
  1. Voting on Election Day

Voting on Election Day

Register to Vote Before Going to the Polls

Registering to vote before you go to the polls is a convenient way to make checking in on election day easier. You can register to vote online.

Check to See if You Are Already Registered to Vote

If you want to check to see if you are already registered to vote, it can easily be done online. Visit the Office of the Minnesota Secretary of State's website to find your registration.

Register to Vote at Your Polling Place on Election Day

You can easily register to vote at your polling place on election day. You'll need to bring proof of residence, such as an ID with your current name and address, or there are several other ways to prove residency. A full list can be found on the Secretary of State website.

Where Do I Vote

To find out where you vote on election day, you can search the polling place finder. This will provide the name and location of where you vote.

What's on My Ballot

Curious about what is on your ballot? Search the My Ballot tool to find a sample ballot.

Other Ways to Vote

Want to vote early? Not a problem. There are many other ways to cast your ballot. Visit the Early Voting page on our website or visit the Office of the Secretary of State for more information.

  1. File for Candidacy

Filing for Office Is How You Get Your Name on the Ballot


Robbinsdale offices include:

  • Mayor
  • City Councilmembers

Filing process:

  • Submit an Affidavit of Candidacy (PDF) to the City Clerk's office during filing dates
  • Provide the $30 filing fee and proper identification when filing

Additional Information:

  • Campaign Filing Packet
  • Office of the Secretary of State Campaign Manual
  • A tool kit will be provided when you file in person
  • Visit the File for Office page on the Hennepin Counties website
  • Email Chase Peterson-Etem, City Clerk

  1. Register to Vote

The easiest way to register is online by visiting the Secretary of State's website. You can also download a paper application on the website. Applications in other languages are also available on the Secretary of State's website. Paper applications are available at City Hall.

  1. Early Voting

During election seasons, early voting is available at City Hall. As a result of recent Minnesota election law changes, the city will implement 18 days of in-person early voting beginning with the 2026 election cycle.

For more information, visit our Early Voting page.

  1. Campaign Signs

Campaign signs are permitted by Minnesota Statute 211B.045 which allows noncommercial signs of any size to be posted in any number beginning 46 days before the state primary in a state general election year until ten days following the state general election. Campaign signs should be removed from properties 10 days after the state general election.
