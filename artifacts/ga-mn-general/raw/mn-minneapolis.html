What's on the ballot

Get a sample ballot before the election so you'll know what you'll be voting on.

See what your ballot will look like

You can see what you'll be voting on ahead of time. You can bring your sample ballot with you when you vote at your polling place.

Get a sample ballot from the Minnesota Secretary of State

General Election

November 3, 2026

Offices on the ballot

  • U.S. Senator
  • U.S. Representative
  • Governor and Lieutenant Governor
  • Secretary of State
  • State Auditor
  • Attorney General
  • State Senator
  • State Representative
  • County Commissioner (Districts 2, 3, 4)
  • County Sheriff
  • County Attorney
  • Minneapolis School Board
    • District seats (Districts 1, 3, 5)
    • At-Large (two seats)
  • Judicial seats
  • Ballot questions, if any
