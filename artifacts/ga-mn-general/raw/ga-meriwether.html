Elections

Overview

The office’s responsibilities include:

  • Assisting elected officials and candidates in completing state ethics forms and maintaining them for public inspection
  • Conducting elections for federal, state, and local offices
  • Federal, state, and local redistricting
  • Maintaining U.S. Justice Department compliance and implementing federal and state legislation, Supreme Court Decisions, and Attorney General Opinions
  • Other functions include voter registration, voter education, and service to the community where we work and live

The diverse office staff consists of 1 full-time Elections Supervisor and a varying number of part-time staff to meet fluctuating work demands and polling place staffing.

ELECTION NOTICE


This is to serve as notice that the preparation and testing of the Electronic Poll Books, Electronic Ballot Markers, Printers, and Ballot Scanners to be used in the November 3, 2026 General Election will commence on Monday, September 28, 2026, and continue daily until complete. The preparation and testing will begin at 8:30 a.m. The testing will take place at the Meriwether County Board of Elections office located at 756 Woodbury Hwy Suite B, Greenville, GA.

Representatives of political parties and bodies, candidates, news media and the public are invited to observe the test.

 

 Patty Threadgill 

 Supervisor of Elections,

NOTICE AND CALL OF SPECIAL ELECTION FOR MERIWETHER COUNTY BOARD OF EDUCATION DISTRICT 4

 

 

Notice is hereby given that a vacancy in the seat for District 4 of the Meriwether County Board of Education has occurred and that a special election to fill said vacancy has been called.   Said vacancy is for the unexpired term of Brad Smith.

 

Said election will be conducted on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, in Meriwether County Board of Education District 4, which is the next available date for the conduct of a Board of Education Special Election.  A run-off election, if needed will be held on Tuesday, December 1, 2026.  The voting polls shall be open at 7 o’clock A.M., and shall close at 7 o’clock P.M., on said date fixed for said election.

 

The locations for voting will be at all regular voting precincts in District 4, Meriwether County Board of Education Election.  Only voters registered to vote in Meriwether County Board of Education District 4 may vote in said election.  

 

All persons desiring to qualify as candidates shall qualify at the Office of the Meriwether County Board of Elections and Registration, 756 Woodbury Hwy, Suite B, Greenville, Georgia.   The first date on which candidates may qualify to run in said election shall be Monday, March 2, 2026, beginning at 9:00 A.M. and ending at 12:00 P.M. on Friday, March 6, 2026.

 

The qualifying fee shall be $414.00; however, candidates may qualify as paupers in accordance with the law.   

 

The last date in which to register to vote in said election shall be on or before Monday, October 5, 2026.

 

This the 5th day of February, 2026.
