Election Judge Information

Serving as an election judge is an excellent way to get involved within your community and allows you to learn election procedures and ensure that elections are administered in a fair manner.

Election Judges are assigned to the city or township in which they live.  Please fill out the election judge interest form below and return to the Carlton County Auditor's office via mail, email or in person and we will forward to the correct jurisdiction.
