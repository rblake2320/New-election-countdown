Election and Voter Information

Tuesday November 3rd 2026

General Election

Find out where to vote for the General Election. Find out what candidates and races are up for election by viewing a sample ballot. You can vote:

  • by mail by requesting an absentee ballot
  • early at the Beltrami County Elections office starting Friday, October 16th from 8:00am to 4:30pm (Monday to Friday) 
  • early at the Beltrami County Elections office on Tuesday, October 27th from 8:00am to 7:00pm
  • in person at the Beltrami County Elections office Saturday, October 24th, 31st, and Sunday, November 1st from 9:00am to 3:00pm
  • at additional one-day early voting pop-up locations 
    • BSU Hobson Memorial Union, Beaux Arts Ballroom (1500 Birchmont Dr NE, Bemidji) on Tuesday, October 20th and Wednesday, October 21st from 11:00am to 3:00pm
    • Leech Lake Mission Community Center (2126 Finch SE, Cass Lake) on Friday, October 23rd from 4:00pm to 8:00pm
    • Old Red Lake Community Center (15041 Hwy 1 W, Red Lake) on Wednesday, October 28th from 12:00pm to 6:00pm
  • on Election Day at your polling place on Tuesday, November 3rd from 7:00am* to 8:00pm 

*Polling place opening hours may differ by location.

Frequently Asked Questions

I am going to be out of town on Election Day; can I still vote?

Yes, you can vote by mail or vote in person.

I am not registered; can I still vote on Election Day?

Yes, Minnesota offers same day registration. Be sure to bring a photo ID and proof of residence to your polling place.

I am a college student; how do I vote?

You must first register at an address; for many students that is their school residential address or their parent's house. You can vote in person on Election Day or vote by mail. 

Election Responsibilities

This office administers federal, state, and county elections, as well as assists in city, township, and school district elections. Responsibilities include:

  • Process voter registration applications
  • Program ballots and test voting equipment
  • Issue absentee ballots
  • Train and certify election officials
  • Compile and report election results
