November 3 2026 General Election Information

Elections Posted on September 24, 2026

  • "Sample Ballot for November 3, 2026 General Election (PDF)" 

  • "Certified Write-In Candidates November 3, 2026 (PDF)" is the official list of qualified write-in candidates published by the Secretary of State's Elections Division. This list specifies which write-in candidates will have their votes counted for the U.S. Senate, Governor, and U.S. House District 11 races.   

  • "Constitutional Amendments Summary Nov 3, 2026 (PDF)" contains the full text of the three proposed constitutional amendments that appear on the sample ballot. It also provides short summaries of each amendment.  

  • Daily Absentee Reporting for the November 3, 2026 General Election

Last day to register to vote or to update your name or address on your voter registration

The last day to register to vote or to change your name or address on your voter registration is on Monday, October 5th.  You can register to vote in person in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office or online at My Voter Page

Early/Advanced Voting 

Early voting for the November 3, 2026 General Election will begin on Tuesday, October 13th and will end on Friday, October 30th.  All early voting is held in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office, located in the Gordon County Courthouse on the 1st floor, 100 S Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia.  Monday - Friday from 8:30 am until 5 pm.  

Saturday voting will be on October 17th and October 24th in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office, office hours are from 9 am until 5 pm

Absentee by Mail Voting

  • First Day to request an absentee by ballot:  August 17, 2026
  • Last Day to request an absentee by mail ballot:  October 23, 2026

To request an absentee ballot call the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office at 706-629-7781 or email:  elections@gordoncountyga.gov

Election Day

Election Day is November 3, 2026, polls will be open from 7 am until 7 pm.  To find out where you vote on Election Day call the Gordon County Board of Elections and Voter Registration Office at 706-629-7781 or visit My Voter Page

Public Notice - Computation and Canvassing Notice for November 3, 2026 General Election

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-492, notice is hereby given that the computation and canvassing of the returns of votes cast in the November 3, 2026 General Election will take place following the close of the polls, in the office of the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office, located at 100 S Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia, beginning at 7:00 p.m. until all absentee ballots received by the close of polls, including those cast by advance voting, and all ballots cast on the day of the election have been counted and tabulated and the results of such tabulation released to the public and, then, continuing with provisional ballots as provided in Code Sections 21-2-418 and 21-2-419 and those absentee ballots as provided in subparagraph (a) (1) (G) of Code Section 21-2-386 from day to day until completed. All public is welcome to attend. This is the 29th day of September, 2026. 

Public Notice - Intent to Process and Tabulate Absentee Ballots Early for the November 3, 2026 General Election

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-386(a)(2)(A) and State Election Board Rule 183-1-14-.14 this is to notify the citizens of Gordon County that the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration will begin the processing of absentee ballots cast in the November 3, 2026 General Election at 4:30 p.m. on November 3, 2026. Said processing shall be open to the view of the public and will take place in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office located in the Gordon County Courthouse at 100 S. Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia.

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 21-2-386(a)(3), this is to notify the citizens of Gordon County that the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration will begin the tabulation of absentee ballots cast in the November 3, 2026 General Election at 4:30 p.m. on November 3, 2026. Said tabulation shall take place in the Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration Office located in the Gordon County Courthouse at 100 S. Wall Street, Suite 1, Calhoun, Georgia. The process for tabulating absentee ballots prior to 7:00 p.m. shall be conducted in a manner to maintain the secrecy of all ballots and to protect the disclosure of any balloting information before 7:00 p.m. on Election Day.

For additional information you may contact the Board of Elections and Registration office at 706-629-7781.

Submitted by: Shea Hicks, Chairperson, Gordon County Board of Elections and Registration
