  1. How To Vote

The city clerk is available to answer questions about voter registration and elections. The city clerk administers city, state, and federal elections in even years.

Check if you are registered to vote

  • Find Your Minnesota Voter Registration

Pre-register to vote

  • Submit an online registration
  • Mail in a paper registration
  • Update your Minnesota Driver's License or Identification Address when you renew your license.

Deadline for pre-registration

  • The deadline to pre-register for the General Election is October 13, 2026
    (Extended hours from 8:00 am -- 5:00 pm; 11:59 pm if pre-registering online).

If you are not pre-registered to vote by the deadline, you can still register same day during the Early Voting window or on Election Day at your polling location.

Learn more about Election Day Registration.

Get an Accessible Ballot

Voters with print disabilities may request an accessible absentee ballot. To do so, apply normally online or with a downloadable application, then contact Hennepin County Government Center to let them know you want an accessible ballot.

  1. In-Person Absentee Voting

Absentee voting is the process of voting before election day and begins 46 days prior to the election. Any eligible voter can absentee vote. Hennepin County assists the City of Richfield with absentee voting. Residents who wish to vote early by absentee ballot or direct ballot (effective in 2026, direct ballot is now referred to as Early Voting) for any election should follow the provisions. Each process requires voters to complete an application process.

Absentee Voting by Mail

Residents may still request a ballot be delivered to them and can apply online available mid-May for elections taking place in August and November. Apply at least three weeks before an election to allow enough time for your application to be processed, for your ballot to be sent to you, and for you to return your ballot.

Visit the Secretary of State website at: https://sos.mn.gov/elections-voting/other-ways-to-vote/online-absentee-application/.

  1. A voter fills out an absentee ballot application.
    • This application can be filled out online or with a paper absentee ballot application (PDF).
      • Online applicants will need to provide an email address.
      • Paper applications should be mailed or emailed directly to:
        Hennepin County Government Center
        300 South 6th Street MC 680
        Minneapolis, MN 55487-0012
        Email: [email protected]
    • Voters need to provide the last 4 of their social security number or Minnesota Driver's License / ID Number.
  2. The voter receives the ballot.
    • A ballot and materials are mailed to the voter from Hennepin County.
      • Be sure to follow the instructions provided!
      • A witness is required. A witness can be a Notary Public or a Minnesota resident.
  3. Return the voted ballot. The ballot must be received before 5 pm on Election Day.
    • Use the pre-paid, pre-addressed envelope to Hennepin County.
    • Election mail is high priority to the U.S. Post Office; however, you may choose to express courier or hand-deliver your ballot to Hennepin County.
    • Drop off your own ballot at the Richfield Municipal Center during regular business hours. You may drop off ballots for up to three other voters by completing a Voter Drop Off Form. Voted ballots are securely locked and couriered to Hennepin County for Absentee Ballot Board review and acceptance.
  4. Track my ballot
    • You can track your ballot during absentee voting. View the Secretary of State Ballot Tracker.

In-Person Absentee Voting in-Person Begins at Hennepin County Government Center September 18, 2026

This is identical to getting an absentee ballot mailed to you, except done in person at the Hennepin County Government Center. You will need to know your MN ID or SSN to fill out the application. In-Person Absentee Voting begins September 18, 2026, and continues for 28 days until the Early Voting period begins on October 16.

IN-PERSON ABSENTEE VOTING LOCATION:
Hennepin County Government Center
300 South 6th St, Skyway Level
Minneapolis, MN 55487-0012
612-348-5151
Email: [email protected]

  1. A voter fills out an absentee ballot application.
    • During the first 28 days of absentee voting, voters will either need to provide the last 4 of their social security number or Minnesota Driver's License / ID Number when completing the in-person ballot application. Election staff do not need to see your identification unless you are an unregistered voter or if the identifying information in the Minnesota voter registration system does not match.
  2. Hennepin County election staff will check you in and issue a ballot to you.
    • During the first 28 days of the absentee voting period, there will be a series of envelopes that will protect your voting privacy.
    • During the early voting period (previously direct balloting period and 18 days prior to election day), no envelopes will be used.
  3. Return the ballot.
    • During the 28-day absentee voting period, election staff will verify that you've signed the signature envelope and you have a witness signature, which should be the person who can verify you completed a blank ballot, is 18 years of age, and a US citizen. Once your ballot is returned, it will be securely locked, and reviewed by an absentee ballot board.
  4. Track my ballot.
    • You can track where your ballot during absentee voting with the Secretary of State Ballot Tracker.

  1. Early Voting

The City of Richfield is pleased to announce the implementation of an 18-day early voting period for upcoming elections. Beginning with the Primary Election Early Voting Period, voters in Richfield will begin voting early 18 consecutive days before election day. This process is similar to voting in a polling place on Election Day. 

EARLY VOTING DETAILS

  1. Voter completes a Voter’s Certificate
  2. Ballot is issued at the Richfield Municipal Center
  3. After ballot completion, ballot is inserted directly into a tabulator

EARLY VOTING DATES AND HOURS:

  • Monday–Friday: 8:00 am – 4:30 pm (October 16, 2026 - November 2, 2026)
  • Saturday: 9:00 am – 3:00 pm (October 24, 2026)
  • Tuesday: 8:00 am -- 7:00 pm (October 27, 2026)
  • Saturday: 9:00 am – 3:00 pm (October 31, 2026)
  • Sunday: 9:00 am - 3:00 pm (November 1, 2026)
  • Monday: 8:00 am – 5:00 pm (November 2, 2026)

EARLY VOTING LOCATION:

Richfield Municipal Center
Council Chambers
6700 Portland Avenue South
Richfield, MN 55423

WHO CAN VOTE EARLY?
Any eligible voter registered in Richfield may vote during the 18-day early voting period.

WHAT TO BRING

  • Minnesota driver's license, state ID, or other acceptable identification (if required)
  • If you need to register at the time of voting, please bring proof of residence

ELECTION DAY

November 3, 2026

Polls Open: 7:00 am – 8:00 pm

  1. At your polling place on Election Day
  2. Vote absentee (early) by mail
  3. Vote absentee (early) in-person

June 26 Through August 10, 2026

Early Voting, previously Direct Balloting (inserting ballot directly into the ballot counter) begins, Friday, July 24, 2026.
Agent Delivery begins on Tuesday, August 4, 2026.

Richfield's Early Voting Extended Hours: 

  • Saturday, August 8, 2026, 9:00 am -- 3:00 pm
  • Monday, August 10, 2026, 8:00 am -- 5:00 pm

August 11, 2026

Polling places are open from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm. You have a right to take the time off you need from work to vote.

There are three ways you can vote in Minnesota:

  1. At your polling place on Election Day
  2. Vote absentee (early) by mail
  3. Vote absentee (early) in-person

In-Person Absentee Voting at Hennepin County Government Center for General Election

September 18 through November 2, 2026

In-Person Absentee voting begins September 18, and is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm the Hennepin County Government Center, 300 S 6th Street Minneapolis, MN 55487 (Skyway Level). Please see Hennepin County’s website for more information or call Hennepin County Elections at 612-348-5151 with questions.

Absentee Voting Assistance at Richfield Municipal Center

September 18 through November 2, 2026

Staff will be available in the City Council Chambers from 8-4:30 PM Monday through Friday to assist residents with registration questions, returning a completed mail-out ballot, or requesting a mail-out ballot from Hennepin County. Residents cannot vote in-person in Richfield until October 16.

Early Voting at Richfield Municipal Center for General Election

October 16 through November 2, 2026

Early voting is available to Richfield residents at the Richfield Municipal Center beginning October 16 from 8:00 am– 4:30 pm, Monday through Friday. Early Voting is formerly known as Direct Balloting (inserting ballot directly into the ballot counter). Richfield also offers select days with extended voting hours.

  • Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 8:00 am -- 5:00 pm (Absentee voting assistance includes completing online request for mail-out ballot /online registration/returning of completed mail-out ballot.)
  • Saturday, October 24, 2026, 9:00 am – 3:00 pm (Early Voting period.)
  • Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 8:00 am -- 7:00 pm (Early Voting period.)
  • Saturday, October 31, 2026, 9:00 am – 3:00 pm (Early Voting period.)
  • Sunday, November 1, 2026, 9:00 am – 3:00 pm (Early Voting period.)
  • Monday, November 2, 2026, 8:00 am – 5:00 pm (Early Voting period.)

Agent Delivery begins seven days before the Election on Tuesday, October 27, 2026.
