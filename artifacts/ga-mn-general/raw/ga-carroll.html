Sample Ballots

The sample ballot is for informational use only and will be available approximately three weeks before the election.  You can mark portions of the sample ballot to use as a guide when you vote. 

Important Notes:

  • Remember, you must be registered to vote by the voter registration deadline in order to vote in any election.
    • The Registration Deadline for the November 2026 General Election is October 5, 2026.
  • Sample Ballots show ALL races, contests, and questions. Depending on where you live in the county, your ballot may not have every race listed on the composite sample ballot. If you want to view the exact ballot you will see on Election Day, visit the Georgia My Voter Page. 

To confirm your voter registration, find your precinct location, or to view your exact sample ballot, visit Georgia MVP.
